Profile

AI Security Maturity Model (AISMM), draft 0.1. The structure follows the CISA Zero Trust Maturity Model: dimensions play the role of pillars, elements play the role of functions, and there are three cross-cutting capabilities.

Thresholds in acceptance criteria are starting points. Calibrate them to your organization's risk appetite before running an assessment.
